# Risks

Purpose: risks to our delivery, not risks in the client's process. Impacts
on their process belong in the wiki as knowledge.
Done when: never. Reviewed at every plan sync and every gate.

This file is authored, unlike `assumptions.md` and `open-questions.md`. It
is the one register nothing else generates.

P and I are probability and impact: H, M, L.

Statuses: open, accepted, mitigated, closed, materialised. Accepted means
we are not acting on it and have decided that consciously.
