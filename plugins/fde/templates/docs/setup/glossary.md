## Process terms

Pre-filled; these mean the same on every engagement.

- **Tier** — the authority of a source: `statutory` > `artefact` >
  `stated` > `ours`. Assigned once in `raw/ledger.md`, carried by every
  derived claim.
- **ADR** — architecture decision record, in `project/decisions/`. One
  decision, its rejected alternatives, its consequences.
- **Gate** — a phase boundary with an evidence checklist in
  `project/gates/`. Self-signed, links only, auditable by anyone.
- **Assumption** — a thing treated as true without a citable source.
  Tagged at the point of use, aggregated in `project/assumptions.md`.
- **Finding** — a lesson about running engagements, not about this
  client's domain. `project/findings.md`.
- **D / AI / A / H / X** — the step classification: deterministic
  software, model reasoning, agent with tools, human, remove entirely.
- **FDC** — the forward deployed consultant. Keeps the record true and
  cuts the milestones.
- **AIE** — the AI engineer. Builds the milestones.
- **Delivery** — a date and a scope promised to the client.
- **Milestone** — a vertical slice of the system. Ours, not the client's.
