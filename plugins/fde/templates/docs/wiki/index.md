# Wiki

Purpose: the knowledge home. Business processes, rules, requirements —
one home per fact.
Done when: never. `raw/` is what we received; this is what we understand.

Rules: no unsourced facts. Cite the meeting or the file and page, or tag
`(assumption)` and mirror it into `project/open-questions.md`. Same term,
same meaning, everywhere (`setup/glossary.md`).

## Contents

[`process.md`](process.md) and [`data-sources.md`](data-sources.md) are
stamped empty and filled by `/fde:fdc-session`. They are flat nodes carrying
`tier: ours`, since they hold what the FDC understood from interviews
rather than what a document said.

Everything else grows from the material, not ahead of it. `/fde:file`
creates and owns the rest of the structure here. Its expensive layer may
supersede content in those two nodes with `artefact`-tier material, under
the normal tier rules.

Every run is recorded in [the wiki log](log.md), and every source it drew
on is tracked in [the sources index](sources/index.md).
